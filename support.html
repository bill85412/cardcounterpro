Frequently asked questions

How do I start a new shoe?

Tap New shoe, then tap it again within three seconds to confirm. All entered cards are cleared and the count resets to zero.

I tapped the wrong card.

Tap Undo last to remove the most recent card. You can undo repeatedly.

How is the true count calculated?

True count = running count ÷ decks remaining. Hi-Lo counts 2–6 as +1, 7–9 as 0, and tens and aces as −1.

How are bets suggested?

One unit at a true count of +1 or lower, then two more units for each true count above +1, up to the maximum bet you choose.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Everything runs on your device, including in airplane mode.

Will my data sync between devices?

No. Your shoe and settings stay on the device where you entered them.
