Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Card Counter Pro does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

What the app stores

The cards you enter and your settings (number of decks, bet unit, maximum bet and strategy table rules) are saved only on your device so the app can restore them when you reopen it. This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Network access

The app works entirely offline. It makes no network requests and does not use any server, third-party service, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Device permissions

The app does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
