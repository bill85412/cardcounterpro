# Card Counter Pro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Card Counter Pro does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

- The app works entirely offline. It makes no network requests and has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
- The cards you enter and your settings (number of decks, bet unit, strategy rules) are saved only on your device so the app can restore them when you reopen it. Deleting the app deletes this data.
- The app does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.
